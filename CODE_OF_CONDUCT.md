# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, productive place for contributors and users.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people
- accept feedback professionally
- avoid harassment, insults, and discriminatory language
- respect maintainer decisions about project scope and direction

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or threats
- discriminatory comments
- personal attacks
- publishing private information without permission
- repeated disruption of project discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, block users, or take other reasonable action to protect the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub.
